Rules the design obeys
The card never scrolls Six rows share the space left after header, day strip and tab bar. Rows compress; the card does not grow.
One line per definition Definitions are clipped, not wrapped. Machine text that sprawls visibly loses its tail, so long text looks wrong.
Nothing happens unasked The card is pressed into being. Discover proposes only on request. Insights are asked for.
Type carries the interface Serif for words and meanings, mono for anything the machine counts. No icons in the tab bar — four words and a dot.
Thumb reach Every target is at least 44 px tall. Primary actions sit in the lower third; back sits top-left where a swipe would also work.
Sample data The prototype is set to 18 September 2026, six weeks into a habit started on 8 August 2026.
